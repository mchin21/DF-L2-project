## Data visualization - hit rates tests:
- After clicking "Run All" (or after sequentially running the first three blocks of code), two bar graphs will populate
- The titles of the bar graph are "DF Performance ESL" and "DF Performance English L1"
- Each graph has two categories: Easy Words and Hard Words
- Each category has two bars: a blue bar (R-items) and an orange bar (F-items)
- Each bar has an error bar indicating the standard deviation from the mean

## Data visualization - false alarm rates tests:
- After clicking "Run All" (or after sequentially running the first four blocks of code), two bar graphs will populate
- The titles of the bar graph are "False Alarm Rates (New Items) - English L1" and "False Alarm Rates (New Items) - ESL"
- Each graph has two categories: Easy Words (blue) and Hard Words (orange)
- Each bar has an error bar indicating the standard deviation from the mean

## Descriptive statistics tests:
- After clicking "Run All", descriptive statistics for each participant group prints above the
corresponding bar chart
- Each readout contains the average, standard deviation, and the number of files read for each analysis
